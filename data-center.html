Data center

An optional connection between SEOPub and your own Google Sheet, so the sheet always knows what your store looks like and which of your changes are live.

Optional. SEOPub works exactly the same without it.

What it is

A data center is a Google Sheet with a small script that a team uses to manage SEO work across a whole catalogue. It lists every product, how it performs in Google Search, which products to work on next, and the status of each one.

  1. Send catalogue. SEOPub reads every product from your Salla dashboard (name, SKU, stock, link, meta title and description, description, categories) and sends it to your sheet.
  2. After every product run. SEOPub reads the products it just changed again and sends them, so the sheet can confirm that what Salla saved matches what you published.

Why it goes through SEOPub

Store platforms protect their pages from automated requests, and requests from Google's servers, where sheet scripts run, are usually refused. SEOPub already works inside the dashboard you're logged in to, so it is the one place that can read your products reliably and without workarounds. The sheet receives the data instead of trying to fetch it.

It also means the sheet checks your work against what the store actually saved, not against a cached public page.

What you need

  1. A data center sheet whose script is published as a web app. The sheet shows its address and key on its control panel.
  2. SEOPub 0.0.10 or newer.
  3. A Salla store, using the New dashboard option in SEOPub. Sending the catalogue works with Salla only for now.

The data center sheet itself isn't public yet. This page is for teams that already have one.

Connect it

  1. Open SEOPub. On the first screen, open Data center (optional).
  2. Paste the web app address and the key from your sheet's control panel.
  3. Click Save. Your browser asks once for permission to reach script.google.com. Allow it.

Send your catalogue

  1. Open your Salla dashboard, switched to the store you want to send, and refresh the tab once. Keep only that store's dashboard open, since all Salla stores share the same dashboard address.
  2. In SEOPub, choose Salla and the New dashboard option, then click Send catalogue.
  3. Leave the Salla tab open until the line under the button says Catalogue sent. A store with several thousand products takes about ten to fifteen minutes. You can keep working in other tabs.

Send it again whenever many products change. The sheet shows a reminder when its copy is more than a week old.

What is sent, and where

  1. Product data goes only to the address you enter, which is your own sheet's script. Nothing is sent to SEOPub or to anyone else.
  2. Nothing is sent while the address and key are empty. Clearing them and clicking Save turns the connection off.
  3. Every request carries your key. The sheet ignores any request with a wrong key, and writes nothing.
  4. If the sheet can't be reached, publishing still works. SEOPub shows a short notice on the results screen instead.

If something goes wrong

  1. The send was interrupted. Click Send catalogue again. The sheet keeps its previous catalogue until a send finishes.
  2. The data center refused the data (bad key). Copy the key again from the sheet's control panel and click Save.
  3. Unknown store. The store isn't switched on in the sheet's settings, or the dashboard is logged in to a different store.
  4. Salla did not answer in time. Refresh the Salla tab, check you're still logged in, and send again.
